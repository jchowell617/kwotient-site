Known weaknesses

  • Incentives are captured at contract level, not per season — OTC reports a deal total. Only the per-game bonus is priced per season, so atRisk2026 counts per-game money alone and incentives qualify a player without inflating the figure.
  • A player cut or traded after the freeze still sits in the cohort, by the freeze rule. His forfeited money is a real outcome, not a data error.
  • Per-game bonuses are read from the 2026 season row of the contract as captured on 2026-08-31. A restructure after that date is not reflected.
